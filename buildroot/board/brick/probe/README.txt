TrimUI Brick panel probe

Finds out which screen panel is in your Brick. It changes nothing on the
Brick: it runs only from this card and never touches the Brick's own storage.

1. Write brick-panel-probe.img to a spare SD card (balenaEtcher or similar).
   Everything on that card is replaced.
2. With the Brick off, put the card in and turn the Brick on.
3. After a few seconds the screen shows the panel. Take a photo of it.
   If the screen stays dark, wait 10 seconds anyway: the answer is saved
   to the card either way.
4. Press the power button to turn the Brick off. Take the card out and put
   your usual card back.
5. Send the photo, or the file panel.txt from this card (it is next to this
   README), to whoever gave you the probe.
